# Business Questions of Olist E-Commerce Project

These are the questions I wanted to answer before I started working with the data.
Every chart, query, and pivot table in this project is connected to one of these.
__________________________________________________________________

# 1. Delivery Delays
Which states have the longest delivery delays? Do longer delays lead to worse review scores?

# 2. Revenue
Which product categories bring in the most revenue? Which ones have the highest average order value?

# 3. Customer Satisfaction
What's the average review score in each state? What seems to be causing low scores (like late delivery, product type)?

# 4. Payments
What payment methods do customers use the most? Does paying in more installments mean people spend more?

# 5. Sellers
Which seller states have the best delivery times and review scores?
