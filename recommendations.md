# Recommendations for Olist E-Commerce Project

Simple write-up of what I found and what I think Olist should do about it.
_____________________________________________________________________

# 1. Delivery Delays

**What I found:** Some states have much bigger gaps between the estimated delivery date and the actual delivery date than others. For example, states like RS and MG showed a bigger gap than states like AL or SE. Review scores were a bit lower in some of the states with bigger delivery delays.

**Why it matters:** If customers are told one delivery date but the experience is very different from that, it can affect how happy they are with the order even if the package arrived fine.

**What Olist should do:** Olist should look closely at the states with the biggest gap between estimated and actual delivery. Either speed up logistics there, or give customers more accurate delivery estimates so the customers satisfaction is higher.
_______________________________________________________________________

# 2. Revenue by Category

**What I found:** A small number of product categories bring in most of the revenue. Some categories have lots of orders but low prices(high volume), while others have few orders but high prices(high value).

**Why it matters:** We cant treat every category as equal a "big revenue" category might need very different attention depending on whether it's high-volume or high-value.

**What Olist should do:** Olist should focus on marketing for the high-volume categories, and make sure high-value categories (like furniture or electronics) get careful handling and shipping, as those orders are more expensive to lose or damage.
_______________________________________________________________________

# 3. Customer Satisfaction

**What I found:** Review scores are lower in states with longer delivery delays. This shows delivery speed is an important factor of how customers feel about their order.

**Why it matters:** If delay is a major cause of low scores, fixing delivery issues could directly improve customer happiness and satisfaction.

**What Olist should do:** Olist should prioritize delivery improvements in the lowest-scoring states first. Also check if certain product categories get low scores everywhere which would point to a product issue, not a delivery issue.
_______________________________________________________________________

# 4. Payment Behavior

**What I found:** Most customers pay by credit card, and many choose to pay in installments. Orders paid in more installments tend to have a higher order value.

**Why it matters:** Installments seem to help customers afford bigger purchases. This is useful to know for pricing and promotions.

**What Olist should do:** Olist should consider offering installment options on payment types that don't currently have them, since it may help increase average order size.
_______________________________________________________________________

# 5. Seller Performance

**What I found:** Some seller states consistently have faster deliveries and higher review scores than others.

**Why it matters:** Not all sellers perform the same way, some are doing a better job for the customers than the others.

**What Olist should do:** The management should feature or promote sellers from the top-performing states on the platform. For lower-performing seller states, they can offer support to help them improve.
______________________________________________________________________

# SUMMARY
Delivery speed is a theme across almost every question, itnot only affects review scores but also likely affects how customers feel about sellers too. If Olist's management wants the single biggest improvement, **reducing delivery delays** or being more accurate about delivery estimates can make the most impact.
